## Overview

The portfolio presents Joshua Klein Malonda as a full-stack developer. Its visual language is a dark, precise engineering workspace: terminal cues, monospaced metadata, restrained surfaces, hairline borders, and small emerald availability signals.

## Colors

Use near-black backgrounds and zinc neutrals as the default. White is reserved for primary text and high-priority actions. Emerald communicates active or available status; sky blue is reserved for supporting technology or external-brand accents.

## Typography

Inter carries headings, body copy, and controls. JetBrains Mono carries terminal content, labels, metadata, status indicators, and technical notation. Headings are heavy, tightly tracked, and compact; supporting copy is muted and readable.

## Layout

Keep content within a centered 1160px column with 24px mobile gutters. The hero uses a responsive two-column layout: narrative and actions on the left, elevated terminal demonstration on the right. Sections are vertically stacked with generous breathing room and reveal-on-scroll motion.

## Elevation & Depth

Prefer borders and tonal surface changes over heavy shadows. The terminal is the main elevated object, using a broad, low-opacity black shadow and a subtle inset highlight. Background grid and radial glows remain quiet and subordinate to content.

## Shapes

Use pill shapes for navigation, statuses, tags, and primary actions. Use 12px to 20px radii for cards and terminal surfaces. Borders are 1px hairlines in the border token family.

## Components

- Primary actions use white backgrounds with black text.
- Secondary actions use elevated dark surfaces with a border.
- Navigation is a dark pill with a sliding white active indicator.
- Status indicators use a small pulsing emerald dot and monospaced labels.
- Cards use dark surfaces, hairline borders, and restrained hover brightening.
- The terminal uses familiar window chrome, monospaced content, and staged command output.

## Do's and Don'ts

- Do preserve the dark terminal and engineering identity.
- Do use motion to clarify state or create a sense of live output.
- Do keep contrast, focus outlines, reduced-motion behavior, and readable mobile layouts.
- Do not introduce decorative gradients, saturated palettes, or unrelated typefaces.
- Do not replace the current content or assets with invented claims.
- Do not let the grid, cursor, glow, or terminal effects compete with the portfolio content.
